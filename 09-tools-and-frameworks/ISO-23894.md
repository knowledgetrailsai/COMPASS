# ISO/IEC 23894

*[Home](../INDEX.md) › [09 · Tools & Frameworks](../09-tools-and-frameworks/commercial-platforms.md)*

_Type: Standard (risk management guidance, not certifiable on its own). Issuer: ISO/IEC. Last reviewed: 2026-08-19._

## What it is

Guidance on risk management specifically for AI, adapting the general ISO 31000 risk management approach to AI-specific context — principles, framework, and process for identifying, analyzing, evaluating, and treating AI risk.

## Relationship to ISO/IEC 42001

23894 is guidance that informs how an organization fulfills the risk-management requirements within a 42001 AI Management System — think of 42001 as "what must be in place" and 23894 as "how to do the risk-management part well." Also usable standalone by organizations not pursuing 42001 certification but wanting structured AI risk methodology.

## Core risk management process (ISO 31000-aligned)

1. **Scope, context, criteria**: define the assessment boundary and risk acceptance criteria
2. **Risk identification**: systematically identify AI-specific risk sources
3. **Risk analysis**: understand risk nature, sources, likelihood, consequences
4. **Risk evaluation**: compare against criteria to determine significance and priority
5. **Risk treatment**: select and implement options to modify risk
6. **Monitoring and review**: ongoing, not one-time
7. **Recording and reporting**: documented throughout

This process maps directly onto [04-ai-assurance/AI-risk-assessment.md](../04-ai-assurance/AI-risk-assessment.md) in this repository, which operationalizes the same underlying pattern.

## AI-specific risk sources it highlights

Data quality and bias, model complexity/opacity, autonomy level, the AI system's interaction with humans and other systems, and the broader socio-technical context of deployment — consistent with the risk taxonomy in [01-foundations/risk-taxonomy.md](../01-foundations/risk-taxonomy.md).

## Practical use

Use as the methodological backbone for [04-ai-assurance/AI-risk-assessment.md](../04-ai-assurance/AI-risk-assessment.md) and [03-ai-governance/risk-management.md](../03-ai-governance/risk-management.md) when an organization wants its internal risk process to align with recognized international standards, whether or not full 42001 certification is pursued.
