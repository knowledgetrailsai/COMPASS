# India — Sectoral AI Guidance

*[Home](../../INDEX.md) › [10 · Regulations & Standards](../../10-regulations-and-standards/global-overview.md) › [India](../../10-regulations-and-standards/India/dpdp-act.md)*

_Last reviewed: 2026-08-19 — sectoral guidance in India is issued as circulars/advisories that update periodically; confirm current text with the relevant regulator._

## MeitY advisories

India's Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) has issued advisories to AI companies covering, among other things: labeling/disclosure of AI-generated or synthetic content, avoiding deployment of untested/unreliable AI for consequential use cases without appropriate safeguards, and consent-based mechanisms for content labeling. These are advisory rather than standalone binding legislation, but signal regulatory direction and are increasingly referenced in enforcement/policy discussions — treat as a strong signal of expected practice even where not independently a binding rule.

## RBI (banking and financial services)

The Reserve Bank of India has emphasized responsible AI use in financial services through existing IT/cybersecurity and outsourcing risk-management frameworks, digital lending guidelines relevant to AI-driven credit decisioning, and evolving guidance on algorithmic accountability in lending and fraud detection. Financial-sector AI systems should be assessed against these existing frameworks in addition to the DPDP Act baseline. See [11-sector-specific-ai/financial-services.md](../../11-sector-specific-ai/financial-services.md).

## SEBI (securities markets)

Guidance addressing AI/ML use by market intermediaries, including expectations around algorithm testing, audit trails, and investor protection where AI influences trading or advisory activity.

## IRDAI (insurance)

Guidance touching AI use in underwriting, claims processing, and fraud detection, with an expectation of fairness and non-discrimination consistent with existing insurance regulation principles.

## Practical approach

For any AI system in a regulated Indian sector, treat this file as a starting map, not a complete compliance reference — pull the current circulars/guidance directly from the relevant regulator's website as part of [02-ai-lifecycle/requirements-and-design.md](../../02-ai-lifecycle/requirements-and-design.md), since sectoral guidance in India updates via circular rather than a single consolidated code.
